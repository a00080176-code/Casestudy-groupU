# Conclusion – Synthesis and Broader Implications

To finish, the evolution of mobile phones from Nokia's traditional devices to modern smartphones such as Apple's iPhone represents one of the biggest changes in modern technology. Mobile phones have developed from simple communication devices into powerful tools that influence almost every part of everyday life. By looking at this development from historical, business, social, mathematical and ethical viewpoints, it becomes clear that smartphones have not only changed the way people communicate but have also transformed businesses, society and the technology industry as a whole.

If we look back to the Nokia era, Nokia helped make mobile phones available to all people around the world. In the late 1990s and early 2000s Nokia became most successful phone manufacturer, with reliable phones, low prices and wide range of models. With be able to call and text, customise the design, cellphones became an important part of life, and texting became a popular way for people to keep in touch. Nokia’s fall from the top shows that any company can be overtaken if it doesn’t adapt to major technological changes.

Apple changed the mobile phone world when it made the first iPhone in 2007. It was not just a phone. People could use the internet, listen to music, send messages and touch the screen. In 2008, Apple opened the App Store, where people could download apps. Google also helped by creating Android, which allowed other phone companies to compete. The story of Nokia and Apple shows that companies must keep changing, trying new ideas and listening to what customers want. Being successful in the past does not mean a company will always stay successful.

Smartphones have changed the way businesses make money and work. In the past, phone companies mostly made money by selling phones. Today, they also make money from apps, online shopping, adverts and subscriptions. Smartphones helps businesses reach more people through social media and the internet. They have also created new jobs in areas like app making, online marketing and computer safety. This shows that companies needs to change the way they work to keep up with new technology.

Maths has helped make smartphones better over the years. It helps phones with many things, like taking pictures, using maps and playing games. Maths also helps phones save information and work faster. It is used to make touchscreens work when people touch them. It also helps phones use less battery so they can stay on for longer. This shows maths is not just about numbers and sums. It is also used in everyday life and helps make the technology we use better.

Smartphones have made it much easier for people to talk to each other. People can send messages, make video calls and use social media from anywhere. Phones  help people learn, find information, use maps and do online banking. However, phones can also cause problems. Spending too much time on them can be bad for people, especially young children. Phones can also collect private information about people. Making and throwing away phones can also harm the environment. This shows technology is helpful, but it can also have some bad sides.

In the future, phone companies should try to make phones better for people and the planet. They should make phones that last longer and are easier to fix and recycle. They should also keep people's private information safe. The government should help make sure everyone can use new technology, even people who don't have much money. People should also learn not to spend too much time on their phones. This will help make technology safer, better and easier for everyone to use.

In conclusion, phones have changed a lot over the years. Nokia helped make mobile phones popular, but Apple and other companies made them even better. Today, phones help us talk to people, learn new things, run businesses and use the internet. Phones have made life easier, but they can also cause problems like too much screen time, privacy problems and harm to the environment. The most important thing is that companies keep making phones better and safer for everyone. In the future, phones should help make people's lives easier and better.
