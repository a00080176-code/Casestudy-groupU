# MATHEMATICAL VIEWPOINT ANALYSIS

## Introduction of the Analysis:

The evolution from traditional Nokia mobile phones to modern smartphones , such as the Iphone, 
demonstrates how mathematics has contributed to technologcal development. Older phones were mainly
used for calls and text messages, while modern smartphones combine communication , photography, 
internet access and computing. This evolution can be analysed through exponential growth, binary numbers,
algorithms, geometry and optimisation.

### 1º POINT: Exponential and Computing Growth

Exponential growth helps explain how computing technology has improved over time. Intel explains that Moore's Law describes how the number of transistors on computer chips historically doubled about every two years (Intel, 2025).


The mathematical model is:

**(P(t)=P_0\times2^{t/T})**

Here, (P_0) is the starting amount, (t) is the time passed and (T) is the doubling period.

If the amount doubles every two years, after ten years the calculation would be:

(P(10)=P_0\times2^5=32P_0)

This means the amount would become 32 times larger under this model. However, this does not mean a smartphone is automatically 32 times faster than an older Nokia. It shows how quickly certain aspects of technology can improve. These developments have helped modern phones run more demanding applications and perform more complex tasks.

### 2º POINT: Binary numbers and Data Storage

Computers use binary numbers to store and process digital information. Binary only uses two digits: 0 and 1. According to the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), one byte contains eight bits.

For example, the binary number 1011 represents 11 in decimal:

(1011_(base 2)=8+2+1=11(base 10))

Older Nokia phones had limited storage compared with modern smartphones. They mainly stored contacts, messages and simple games, while today's phones can store thousands of photos, videos and applications.
